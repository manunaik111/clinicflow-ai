# Greenfield Family Clinic: Patient Information

> NOTE: Greenfield Family Clinic is a fictional clinic created for a demo project.
> All details below are synthetic and not real medical or business information.

## Opening Hours
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM
- Sunday and public holidays: Closed

## Location and Contact
- Address: 14 Garden Road, Springfield (fictional)
- Phone: (555) 010-2030
- Email: reception@greenfieldclinic.example
- Free parking is available behind the building.

## Booking and Appointments
- Appointments can be booked by phone, email, or through this assistant.
- Standard consultations last 15 minutes. Long consultations (30 minutes) are available on request.
- Same-day appointments are released every morning at 8:00 AM for non-urgent issues.
- New patients should arrive 10 minutes early to complete registration and bring photo ID.

## Cancellation and Rescheduling
- Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule.
- Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may incur a $25 late-cancellation fee.
- Rescheduling can be requested by phone, email, or through this assistant.

## Fees and Billing
- Standard consultation (15 minutes): $60
- Long consultation (30 minutes): $110
- Children under 16 and pensioners: $40 for a standard consultation.
- Payment is accepted by card or cash at the front desk after the appointment.
- Receipts for insurance claims are provided on request.

## Insurance
- We accept most major private health insurance plans.
- Please bring your insurance card to every visit.
- For coverage questions, contact your insurer directly, as we cannot confirm coverage on their behalf.

## Prescription Refills
- Repeat prescriptions require 48 hours notice.
- A refill request needs the patient's full name, date of birth, and medication name.
- The doctor may require a short review appointment before renewing a prescription.
- Controlled medications cannot be refilled through this assistant.

## Test Results
- Results are usually available within 3 to 5 working days.
- The clinic will contact you if a result needs urgent attention.
- Results are never shared by email. Please call or visit to discuss them.

## Services Offered
- General health check-ups
- Vaccinations and flu shots
- Minor injury treatment
- Chronic condition management (for example, diabetes and blood pressure)
- Women's health and family planning
- Child health and immunisations

## Emergency Policy (IMPORTANT)
- This clinic and this assistant are NOT for medical emergencies.
- If you have chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, thoughts of self-harm, or any life-threatening symptom, call your local emergency number immediately or go to the nearest emergency department.
- This assistant must never give a diagnosis or medical treatment advice. Clinical questions are passed to clinic staff.
